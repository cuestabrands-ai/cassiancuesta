📅 Ideas for This Week
1
Piano + Fractions

Play a piece you know. Count: how many quarter notes, half notes, whole notes? Draw them as fractions. How much of the bar does each one fill?

2
Pattern Hunt in Real Life

Find 3 patterns outside — in tiles, leaves, windows, or music. Write down the rule for each one. Can you create your own pattern?

3
Robot Designer Challenge

Draw a robot on paper. If it needs to turn right, what angle is that? If it takes 5 steps forward and 3 back, where does it end up? Write the math for it!

4
Arabic Number Detective

Research: what did Arabic mathematicians invent? Write 5 cool facts (in English, Spanish, or Arabic — your choice!). Bonus: learn to write numbers 1-10 in Arabic script.
